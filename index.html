Every activity at the meeting placed by room and time: symposia, contributed talk sessions, posters, plenaries, meetings, workshops, socials, and support space. Filter by date, by division, or by activity type, and columns collapse as rooms empty out.

Contributed talk sessions

The sessions as the divisions have drafted them, in their own numbered order, from the session planner rather than from the programmed schedule. So this page works from the moment abstracts start being assigned, months before there is a grid to draw. Nothing here is placed: a drafted session has no room, day, or time. Each card carries the keywords its abstracts share, since a drafted session has no title yet. Filter by division, click a session to list its talks, or switch to similarity mode to find two sessions that cover the same ground.

Poster sessions

One bar per poster session, its length proportional to how many posters the session holds, so the uneven ones are obvious at a glance. Coloured and filtered by division. Time is left out because every poster session runs in the same slot. Click a bar to list its posters.

Symposia

One card per symposium, which is the one part of the meeting the other pages take apart: its three blocks sit in three grid cells, its complementary sessions land on another day in another room, and its complementary posters look like every other poster in the hall. Organizers, every session with its room and times, the subject tags, and the talks on a click. The stacked bars at the top show what each symposium brings, invited and complementary kept separate.

Program statistics

How much of everything there is: talks and posters per division, sessions per division with how tightly each is packed, a division-by-division matrix of how alike their presentations are, the spread of session sizes, and the rooms in use through each day against the size of the room list. Counts only, so no individual is named.

The venue 2027 only

These are the 2027 floor plans, whichever year is selected above. SICB 2027 is at the David L. Lawrence Convention Center in Pittsburgh, 3 to 7 January: all meeting space is in that one building, and the Westin is the room block only. Use them when you are deciding which rooms your division's sessions should go in. Earlier cycles were at other venues, 2026 at the Oregon Convention Center and the Hyatt Regency in Portland, so the room names on a 2026 page will not appear on these plans.
