Contributed talk sessions

The sessions as the divisions have drafted them, in their own numbered order, from the session planner rather than from the programmed schedule. So this page works from the moment abstracts start being assigned, months before there is a grid to draw. Nothing here is placed: a drafted session has no room, day, or time. A drafted session has no title either, so each card is headed by the keywords its abstracts share. Filter by division, then click a session: it lists the talks and recolours every other session by how close its science is, so a second click opens the two side by side. That is how you find one session split across two numbers. The data toggle switches between the draft and what the cycle was actually programmed as.

Poster sessions

One bar per poster session, its length proportional to how many posters the session holds, so the uneven ones are obvious at a glance. Coloured and filtered by division. Time is left out because every poster session runs in the same slot. Click a bar to list its posters.

Symposia

One card per symposium, which is the one part of the meeting the other pages take apart: its three blocks sit in three grid cells, its complementary sessions land on another day in another room, and its complementary posters look like every other poster in the hall. Organizers, every session with its room and times, the subject tags, and the talks on a click. The stacked bars at the top show what each symposium brings, invited and complementary kept separate.

Not built for this year yet Sort by number, size, or day Program content only, no contact details

Program statistics

How much of everything there is: talks and posters per division, sessions per division with how tightly each is packed, a division-by-division matrix of how alike their presentations are, the spread of session sizes, and the rooms in use through each day against the size of the room list. Counts only, so no individual is named.
