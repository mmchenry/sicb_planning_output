Poster sessions

One bar per poster session, its length proportional to how many posters the session holds, so the uneven ones are obvious at a glance. Coloured and filtered by division. Time is left out because every poster session runs in the same slot. Click a bar to list its posters.

The venue

SICB 2027 is at the David L. Lawrence Convention Center in Pittsburgh, 3 to 7 January. All meeting space is in that one building; the Westin is the room block only. Use these when you are deciding which rooms your division's sessions should go in.
