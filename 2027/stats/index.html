Mean similarity between every pair of abstracts across two divisions, from the GPT keyword vectors. Dark is alike, and the scale spans the values in the grid, so the darkest cells are the pairs worth not scheduling against each other.
The diagonal is blank. It is a different quantity, the mean over pairs of two different abstracts within one division, which measures how focused that division's own submissions are rather than how close it sits to another. It also runs several times higher than any pair of divisions, so sharing one colour scale with it left every other cell pale. Those values are listed below the grid instead.
