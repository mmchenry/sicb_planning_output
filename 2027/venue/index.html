SICB 2027 venue

David L. Lawrence Convention Center, Pittsburgh · 3 to 7 January 2027

SICB 2027 is held entirely in the David L. Lawrence Convention Center in downtown Pittsburgh, on the bank of the Allegheny River. All meeting space is in that one building, so there is no shuttle between venues; the headquarters hotel, the Westin, is attached to it.

Addresses

David L. Lawrence Convention Center: 1000 Fort Duquesne Boulevard, Pittsburgh, PA 15222

The Westin Pittsburgh, the headquarters hotel: 1000 Penn Avenue, Pittsburgh, PA 15222

Getting there

Pin on the convention center; the Westin sits immediately to its southwest.

From the Westin. The Westin Pittsburgh adjoins the convention center and connects to it indoors, so the walk is a few minutes without going outside. Leave the hotel toward the convention center entrance on Penn Avenue and follow the signs to registration on Level 2; escalators and elevators run from there up to the Level 3 breakout rooms.

From the airport. Pittsburgh International Airport is about 30 to 40 minutes by car or ride-share to downtown. The 28X Airport Flyer bus runs to downtown and the convention center for a few dollars.

The floors

Level 2: the front of house

Level 2

Where you arrive. Registration, the exhibits, the posters and the largest gatherings are on this floor; it has no session rooms.

  • Hall C: the plenary hall, seating 1,800. Sunday's Science and Society Plenary and the Student and First Timers Orientation are here.
  • Ballroom A-C: registration, the exhibitors, and every poster session.
  • Ballroom Gallery: evening socials, Meeting of the Minds, and the Closing Celebration.

Level 3: the sessions

Level 3

Most of the meeting happens here: the talk sessions, the symposia, the named plenary lectures, and the rooms set aside for delegates.

  • Session rooms 301 to 307 and 317 to 318: contributed talks, Best Student Paper talks, and special and complementary sessions. Several also hold member meetings and workshops at lunch and in the evening.
  • Symposia: the largest in 310-311, the others in 315 and 316.
  • 304-305, opened into one room: the Presidential Plenary, the Bern, Bartholomew and Gans lectures, the Moore Lectureship, and the SICB Member Meeting.
  • Meeting rooms 308, 312 and 319: committee meetings, editorial boards and meetups.
  • For delegates: 309 Speaker Ready Room, 320 Mothers Room, 322 Quiet Room, 313 Meeting Office, 314 AV Office.

Level 4: more session rooms

Level 4

Rooms 401 to 405 hold contributed talks and special and complementary sessions through the day, with some member meetings and workshops. The plan also shows rooms 406 to 415, which SICB does not use.

The Westin: the headquarters hotel

The Westin

Connected to the convention center by an enclosed skywalk. A few events are here: the Sunday Executive Committee meeting in Crawford, socials in Cambria, Fayette and the hotel's own Ballroom A-C (not the convention center's), and the childcare room.

Walking time between rooms

About how many minutes it takes to walk from one room to another, estimated from the floor plans. The scheduler uses these to put sessions on related subjects in rooms near each other. Combined rooms such as 301-302 are left out, since they sit where their halves do. Hover over a cell for the pair.
