SICB 2027 venue

David L. Lawrence Convention Center, Pittsburgh · 3 to 7 January 2027

SICB 2027 is held entirely in the David L. Lawrence Convention Center in downtown Pittsburgh, on the bank of the Allegheny River. All meeting space is in that one building, so there is no shuttle between venues; the headquarters hotel, the Westin, is attached to it.

Address1000 Fort Duquesne Boulevard, Pittsburgh, PA 15222

Getting there

Pin on the convention center; the Westin sits immediately to its southwest.

From the Westin. The Westin Pittsburgh adjoins the convention center and connects to it indoors, so the walk is a few minutes without going outside. Leave the hotel toward the convention center entrance on Penn Avenue and follow the signs to registration on Level 2; escalators and elevators run from there up to the Level 3 breakout rooms.

From the airport. Pittsburgh International Airport is about 30 to 40 minutes by car or ride-share to downtown. The 28X Airport Flyer bus runs to downtown and the convention center for a few dollars.

The floors

Level 2 — the front of house

Level 2

The public heart of the meeting, and where a delegate arrives. It carries no breakout rooms; its spaces are the ones everyone passes through.

  • Hall C — the plenary hall, theater seating for 1,800. The Sunday plenary and the student orientation are here.
  • Ballroom A-C — registration, the exhibitors, and the poster sessions. Not a presentation room.
  • Ballroom Gallery — reception space, where the evening divisional socials are held.

Level 3 — the sessions

Level 3

The core of the programme. Almost every contributed and special session runs here, in the 300-series breakout rooms, alongside the committee rooms and the delegate support spaces. This is the floor you are assigning your division's sessions to.

  • Breakout rooms 301–335 — the contributed, Best Student Presentation, and special sessions. Room 316 takes workshops and member meetings only.
  • Committee rooms 306, 307, 309 — smaller meetings; 309 also holds the daily Pop Up Meeting.
  • Support: 308 Speaker Ready Room, 311 childcare, 312 mothers room, 322 quiet room, 313 meeting office, 314 AV office.

Level 4 — the overflow

Level 4

One room on this floor is part of the SICB programme: Breakout 13 (rooms 401–402), for a larger session. The plan also shows rooms 403–415 and the 406 and 407 lecture rooms, which the resume does not book, so they are shown for orientation rather than as meeting space.

The Westin — the headquarters hotel

The Westin

Attached to the convention center, the Westin is the room block and also carries some meeting space: Crawford takes the Sunday Executive Committee meeting, and Butler, Cambria, and Fayette take workshops and socials. Its own Ballroom A-C is distinct from the convention center's.
